# Daily Operations

## Register found property

Choose **Register found item**, record the required item name, category, date/time found, and storage location, then add useful descriptions, photos, finder information, and internal notes. Keep photos free of unrelated personal information. Save to create a local reference number and custody event.

## Search and filter inventory

Open **Found items**. Search item names, references, descriptions, and related text; filter by status, category, storage area, and found-date range. Use the page controls to move through the results. Open a record to change its details, add or remove photos, move it to another active storage area, or review its custody history.

## Record a lost report

Choose **Lost report** and record the item description, category, approximate date and last location, claimant name, and contact details. Lost reports can be searched, edited, closed, or reopened. Closing a report does not delete its history.

## Review possible matches and claims

Open **Lost reports** and choose **Review matches** on a report. Reclaim ranks possible found items using matching details. A score is only a review aid; staff must compare the evidence and speak with the claimant. Select a candidate to associate the claim, dismiss a suggestion, or remove an association.

If another claimant is already confirmed for the item, Reclaim blocks a competing confirmation and blocks direct return. Review and remove the prior association before confirming another claim. Reclaim does not decide who owns an item.

## Return property

Open the found item and choose **Start return**. Enter the recipient. Link the relevant lost report when applicable. Record the ownership verification method and a short explanation, without storing identification numbers. Verification is required by organization policy or when the item is in secure storage. Confirming a return ends custody and records the action in the history.

The staff name shown in history is a locally configured attribution label. Reclaim does not authenticate staff accounts or enforce roles. Keep the workstation available only to people authorized by your organization.

## Retention and disposition

Open **Retention review** to see items approaching or past their configured review date. Retention dates are reminders for staff; Reclaim never disposes of property automatically and does not determine legal requirements. Review local policy, select the item, and record an explicit disposition method only when authorized. The disposition is retained in custody history.
